Good Habits

Tooth Tips We Actually Teach Kids

🪥

Brush Twice a Day

Two minutes, morning and night — a timer or a favorite song both work great.

🧵

Floss Daily

Even baby teeth need it — cavities between teeth don't show up until they hurt.

🍭

Limit Sugary Snacks

It's less about "never" and more about not sipping or snacking on sugar all day long.

📅

See Us Twice a Year

Regular checkups catch small issues while they're still easy (and cheap) to fix.

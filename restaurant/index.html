Our Philosophy

"Everything on this menu touches live fire. We buy whole animals, harvest produce within a day's drive, and let the wood do most of the talking. Ember & Ash isn't trying to reinvent dinner — it's trying to make it honest again."

— Mara Whitfield, Chef & Owner

Meet the Chef

Mara Whitfield

Chef & Owner, est. 2019

Mara spent six years working the line at acclaimed kitchens up and down the East Coast before opening Ember & Ash in a converted hardware store on Bloomfield Ave. Her food is deliberately unfussy — three or four ingredients per plate, always one of them charred.

The wood-fired hearth runs at the center of the dining room, in view of every table. Nothing on the menu is fried, and almost nothing leaves the kitchen without a pass through the fire first.

Meet Your Dentist

Dr. Elena Cross, DMD

Founder & General Dentist

Dr. Cross has practiced general and cosmetic dentistry in Union County for over a decade. She trained at Rutgers School of Dental Medicine and completed advanced training in sedation dentistry, with a particular focus on patients who've avoided the dentist due to anxiety.

"I tell every new patient the same thing: you're allowed to ask what and why before we do anything. If you understand the plan, you're calmer, and calmer patients heal faster."

01 / CONTEXT

A storefront is not ready when the pages look finished. Orders, stock, staff access, and launch rules must agree.

The client needed a clear public shopping flow and a separate staff area for products, stock, orders, and store settings. Business and health-related workflows also needed strict boundaries before launch.

02 / ENGINEERING DECISIONS
01

Connect the customer and staff journeys.

I built catalog and search views, product pages, a pickup cart, and guest order flows. The admin area manages products, categories, stock, order states, business hours, and publishing from one workspace.

02

Protect the state behind each request.

Checkout calculates totals on the server, verifies quantities, reserves stock atomically, and handles repeated requests. Staff routes check access and use protected sessions; private document routes are not public storefront pages.

03

Make launch gates real.

Pickup ordering depends on approved store details, policies, notifications, and data review. Prescription upload paths remain disabled until the client approves the required hosting and operational checks. No compliance certification is implied.

04

Verify before publishing.

Type checks, build checks, route and domain tests, and mobile browser reviews cover the main flows. DNS activation, real catalog data, email delivery, and final owner approval remain separate launch tasks.

THE TAKEAWAY

For a business app, a safe and clear boundary between built features and approved operations matters as much as the interface.

The codebase is private. This is a pre-launch engineering case study; the custom domain and live order operations are not presented as verified.

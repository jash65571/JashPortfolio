01 / CONTEXT

A reply can be well written and still miss the question. ReplyProof checks what a message asked against what the draft actually answers.

Requests, dates, and attachment promises get buried in email threads. People need a final check close to the draft, without handing over the Send button.

02 / ENGINEERING DECISIONS
01

Keep the interaction small.

The web checker accepts a thread and a draft, then shows what is answered, partial, or missing. The Gmail extension brings that result into the compose flow. The user keeps control of editing and sending.

02

Connect the whole product.

The project includes Stripe Checkout, subscription webhooks, a customer billing portal, license validation, and recovery. The website and extension share the same paid-access model.

03

Make billing states explicit.

The server verifies the expected product and price, payment state, and active subscription before granting a license. Webhook handling accounts for duplicate and stale events. Focused tests cover unpaid checkouts, canceled subscriptions, unknown prices, and older updates.

04

Put boundaries around usage.

Server-side limits cover checks, checkout, billing, recovery, and event routes. Free usage is checked against device and network buckets. Launch totals track key actions without storing email text as inbox history.
